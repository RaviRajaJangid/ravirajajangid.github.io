About Me

Seasoned Mobile Application Developer with 11.5+ years of hands-on expertise in iOS development (Swift, Objective-C) and cross-platform technologies (Flutter, React Native). I have architected and delivered scalable, enterprise-grade applications across fintech, mobility, social networking, and digital credential domains, partnering with innovative startups, leading product-based organizations, and Fortune 500 enterprises.

I currently serve as Delivery Project Lead at Mphasis, directing a high-performing engineering team of 8 professionals on a flagship US banking mobile platform. My specialized expertise encompasses architectural patterns (MVC, MVVM, Clean Swift, VIPER), test-driven development (TDD), SOLID principles, and proven team leadership capabilities with a strong track record of delivering mission-critical applications.

I am committed to harnessing cutting-edge AI tools (GitHub Copilot, Claude, Xcode agents) to elevate development efficiency and code quality. Outside of my professional pursuits, I maintain a balanced lifestyle through fitness training, competitive table tennis, international travel, and swimming.
